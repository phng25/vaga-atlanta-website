📢 VAGA Officially Recognized by GSGA & GHIN — Club Launch Set for December 1st

August 1, 2025

We’re excited to share that we’ve successfully established the Vietnamese Atlanta Golfer Association (VAGA) as an official club through GSGA and GHIN.

However, since most of our members already have active GHIN memberships for the current calendar year, we’ve decided to delay officially activating the club until December 1, 2025. This will help us avoid paying the $1,500 fee for 2025.

The $1,500 fee includes 20 active GHIN memberships and full access to Golf Genius for tournament management. GHIN renewals are $55 per member each year, and if we exceed 20 members, we’ll simply pay the additional GHIN fees per person.

We believe this is a great value—your GHIN profile will be listed under Vietnamese Atlanta Golfer Association, giving our club visibility at any tournament you join. We’re looking forward to launching the VAGA GHIN next year and proudly representing our club wherever we play!
